The fourth Godot version brought a great addition by allowing window management over the entire desktop, event with multiple monitor setups. Taking advantage of this, I made Screequare using it as a main gameplay mechanic: at the start of a game, a square appears at a random position on your screen, which you must clic as fast as possible. Obviously, some complications ought to make the task a challenge. First, the square only stays at a position for a short time before relocating and removing a point from your score; secondly, many restricted regions may appear all over the screen, also removing score if your cursor touches them on the way.
