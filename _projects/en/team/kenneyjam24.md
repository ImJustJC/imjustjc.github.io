<h2>My first GameJam</h2>

<strong>Did you try turning it off and on again?</strong> represents my first try at a GameJam in group made by 2 developers, as an excuse to truly learn Godot. This 2024 KenneyJam defined the theme "Connections", which gave us some ideas until we fixated on a kind of router simulator.

With a clear inspiration on <i>Papers, Please</i>, we tried to reproduce a similar gameplay where you must manage incoming packages like a router, checking its parameters and sending them to a proper destination, making sure source and target directions and ports are correct.

<h2>Design and overscope</h2>

A mix of inexperience and overscoping, added to a short development time, we couldn't finish everything at the end. The basic gameplay works, but without any tutorial or a balance in the game parameters, it ended up more like a proof of concept than a playable game. Nevertheless, building something like it was a great experience and a personal challenge: I never made any graphical user interface like this before, and I managed a proper and functional solution.
